---
title: "Open Knowledge Format"
type: concept
description: "The contract a corpus and its tools agree on, independent of any renderer."
aliases: [OKF]
tags: [fixture]
---

The format is the agreement: a corpus declares its own vocabulary, and every tool that
reads it — the exporter, the checker, the site — enforces the same one.
